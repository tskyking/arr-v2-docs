# Mobile Begin Shift QR refinement

Scope authorized by TK: improve phone scanner, activate the 13 additional printed station codes, provide a place for A+/Admin to validate them. Existing two retained. Public demo update only; stopped rehearsal untouched.

- Phone uses a viewport-sized modal camera with visible Cancel, uncropped preview, and an aiming guide sized within the actual camera image. Desktop uses a bounded large dialog. Whole camera frame is decoded, not only the guide.
- Cancellation, late permission grants, navigation, hidden page, and permission denial stop/release capture. Native detector failures fall back to bundled jsQR. Decode frequency capped and fallback resolution bounded.
- All 15 IDs in the October 9 printed sheet are registered without changing payloads. IDs must remain stable when labels change. This is a static demo registry, not the future server-managed persistent QR catalog.
- A+/Admin: Production → Administration → Station QR validation. Lists all mappings and validates pasted QR text without writing a shift. No activation switch or create/deactivate controls yet.
- Begin Shift remains review-and-submit. A scan preserves the explicit beginning time. Demo readiness shortcut is shown in red and does not synthesize SOP score 10 when a station QR was applied. Full future demo-assumption data model and confirm-time workflow are not implemented in this scoped patch.
- No changeover/break/end-day scanner, password change, schema migration, or rehearsal reset.

Validation: exact printed-mapping fixture (15), malformed/unknown code rejection, TypeScript typecheck, production build, WebKit 390×844 and Chrome 412×915 mocked-camera checks including assignment, cancellation during pending permission, denied permission, decoder fallback, and validation UI. Physical iPhone/Android camera testing remains necessary; viewport/browser-engine checks are not device certification.
